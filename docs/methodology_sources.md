# Authoritative methodology sources

- Van Hove, Dalla Longa & van der Zwaan (2022), "Identifying predictors for energy poverty in Europe using machine learning," *Energy & Buildings* 264, 112064, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2022.112064). ENABLE-EU, country-specific absolute-expenditure Q80, income boundary between brackets 3 and 4, and four risk classes. Published aggregate results are external references only.
- Dalla Longa, Sweerts & van der Zwaan (2021), "Exploring the complex origins of energy poverty in The Netherlands with machine learning," *Energy Policy* 156, 112373, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enpol.2021.112373). Four-quadrant methodological background; Netherlands thresholds are not substituted for the ENABLE rule.
- UK Government, [Fuel Poverty Methodology Handbook 2020: LIHC](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/603fcdaee90e077dd08f15e6/Fuel_Poverty_Methodology_Handbook_2020_LIHC.pdf) and [handbook collection](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/fuel-poverty-statistics-methodology-handbook). Historical LIHC's country-median (~Q50) is cited as one of the two external anchors bracketing the quantile-search bounds (`lib/tuning/quantile_search.py`), alongside Van Hove's Q80; it is not separately computed as a measurement family.
- Copernicus Climate Data Store, [ERA5-Land](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-land).
- Eurostat household [electricity prices, nrg_pc_204](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/nrg_pc_204/default/table?lang=en) and [gas prices, nrg_pc_202](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/nrg_pc_202/default/table?lang=en). Survey-period prices are required.
- Cai et al. (2021), [ARM-Net: Adaptive Relation Modeling Network for Structured Data](https://doi.org/10.1145/3448016.3457321); the screening adapter follows the authors' Apache-2.0 one-head UCI architecture and its supported alpha=1 attention option.
- Eurostat provides harmonised household retail prices for electricity and natural gas, but not a comparable household series for every solid/liquid fuel used in ENABLE. Non-gas/non-electric components therefore remain source-gated to country-level official series.
